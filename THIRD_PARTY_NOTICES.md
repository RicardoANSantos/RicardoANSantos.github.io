# Profile icon attribution

The seven inline profile icons in `index.qmd` come from Font Awesome Free
7.3.1 by Fonticons, Inc.: ORCID, Google Scholar, GitHub, ResearchGate,
Bluesky, X (x-twitter), and LinkedIn (linkedin-in).

Source: https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/7.3.1/svgs/brands
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://fontawesome.com/license/free

The upstream attribution comments are retained in each inline SVG. The website
sets their display size to 18 px and marks them as decorative alongside visible
link names. Most icons inherit the website's forest-green link colour; the ORCID
icon retains the ORCID green (`#a6ce39`) against a white circular background.
The original SVG paths and view boxes are preserved.

All brand icons are trademarks of their respective owners. They identify links
to Ricardo's profiles on those services and do not imply endorsement.
